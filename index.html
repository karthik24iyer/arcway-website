Some questions and
their answers

What does Arcway actually do?

It gives you a live, real terminal session on your Mac from your phone, tuned for coding agents like Claude Code, Codex and Gemini — so long-running tasks don't need you at your desk.

Do I need a VPN or to open ports?

No. Both your Mac and phone connect outbound to the relay, so it works behind NAT, corporate Wi-Fi and cellular.

Is my code sent anywhere?

Sessions are end-to-end encrypted between your phone and your Mac. The relay only forwards bytes and stores nothing.

How much does it cost?

Arcway is free for 1 host and 3 Claude Code sessions. Arcway Forever is a one-time $9.99 purchase — no subscription — that unlocks unlimited hosts and sessions, every agent, and lifetime updates. Self-hosting the relay gets you everything for free.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once and it's yours, including future features. Already bought it? Use Restore Purchases in the app.

What's next?

Voice mode, notifications, phone clipboard, a Linux host, and support for Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf and more. Forever owners get all of it.
